A trivia game for two people who share a couch and not a knowledge base.

Each of you declares the world you know. Every round, you pick the deck your partner gets quizzed on, and they pick yours. Seven questions, fifteen seconds each, and the difficulty climbs from Rookie to Legend over five rounds. First to three crowns wins. Then you run it back.

His World
Football 101, Ball Sports Grab Bag, Cars & Engines, Grilling, Beer & Whiskey, Games & Superheroes, Tools, Gear & Outdoors, Action Movies & Dad TV, Tech & Gadgets, Fight Night.

Her World
Skincare & Beauty, Fashion & Style, Rom-Coms & Love Stories, Reality TV, Pop Divas & Their Eras, Wedding Season, Book Club, Wellness & Self-Care, Celebrity Couples & Gossip.

How it plays
• Spin the wheel to pick your partner's deck, or just tap the slice you want.
• Answer fast: speed and streaks earn bonus points.
• Win the round, take the crown. Ties get nothing. Awkward.
• Ola, your host, keeps score and keeps opinions.

Two ways to play
• Me vs my partner: one phone, passed across the couch.
• Our couple vs theirs: four people, one phone. Each person answers their own world, the other couple picks every deck, and the scores add up. Built for family game night.

648 questions, all fact
Eighteen decks of 36 questions each, from Rookie to Legend. Every question is a checkable public fact, not an opinion. Spot a wrong one? Tell us and we'll fix it.

Nothing to sign up for
No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no internet connection needed, and nothing collected. Your name and your matches stay on your phone.

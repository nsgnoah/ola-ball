Ola

A trivia game for two people who share a couch and not a knowledge base.

His WorldFootball, cars, grilling, tech, fight night
Her WorldSkincare, fashion, rom-coms, reality TV, divas

Declare the world you know, then pick what your partner gets quizzed on. They pick yours. Seven questions a round, fifteen seconds each, first to three crowns. Play from two phones through Game Center, or pass one phone back and forth.

Getting help

Email noah@nsgsolutions.co. Useful things to include: what you were doing, which mode you were in, and your iPhone or iPad model.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Ola has no accounts and no passwords. Playing from two phones uses the Apple ID already signed in to Game Center on your device.
  • A question has the wrong answer. Please tell us, with the question text. Every question is meant to be a checkable public fact, and we will correct it.
  • How do I start over? The menu on the home screen has “Start over”, which clears your profile and pass-and-play matches on that device.
  • How do I remove an online match? Once it has finished, a delete button appears beside it. That removes your copy; the other player keeps theirs.

Privacy

# Ola Ball Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 18, 2026_

Ola Ball does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

- The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
- The app makes no network requests.
- Your progress (chosen team, XP, win/loss record, day streak, and Playbook entries) is stored only on your device, using standard iOS app storage. You can erase it at any time with "Reset progress" on the home screen, or by deleting the app.
- The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or notifications.

If you have questions, contact noah@nsgsolutions.co.
